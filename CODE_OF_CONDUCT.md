# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

Medly is built on the belief that healthcare deserves better. So does our community.

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone — regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity, level of experience, nationality, race, religion, or sexual orientation.

---

## Our Standards

### ✅ Expected

- Treat everyone with respect and dignity
- Welcome newcomers and diverse perspectives
- Give constructive, helpful feedback
- Be patient — especially across time zones and language barriers
- Use inclusive language
- Accept feedback on your own work gracefully

### ❌ Not Tolerated

- Harassment of any kind
- Discriminatory language or slurs
- Personal attacks or insults
- Unwelcome sexual attention
- Sharing private information without consent
- Spam or excessive self-promotion

---

## Health Data Standards

Medly handles sensitive health information. Additional expectations apply:

| Standard | Expectation |
|---|---|
| Privacy | Never ask users to share health data in public channels |
| Medical Advice | Never provide medical advice — direct to professionals |
| Sensitivity | Be considerate when discussing health topics |
| Data Protection | Never handle personal health data outside secure channels |

---

## Reporting

If you experience or witness a violation, open a private issue on [GitHub](https://github.com/Iceman-Dann/Medly/issues).

All reports are handled confidentially. We respond within 48 hours.

---

## Enforcement

| Violation Level | Consequence |
|---|---|
| Minor | Private warning + education |
| Moderate | Temporary suspension |
| Severe | Permanent ban |

Appeals can be submitted to appeals@medly.app within 7 days of any decision.

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all community spaces — GitHub, Discord, email, events, and any official representation of the Medly project.
